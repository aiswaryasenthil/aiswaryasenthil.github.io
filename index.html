👋🏽About

I'm a junior at Cornell University studying Cognitive Science and Information Science, and I'm working toward a PhD in human-computer interaction. I investigate the intersection of cognition and computation to build technology that improves the human experience.

Much of my work so far designs immersive VR and AR interventions for challenges in digital health, accessibility, and education. I'm now expanding into spatial intelligence and AI safety: what do we lose when we hand our thinking off to machines, and how can we build systems that protect people's autonomy instead of replacing their judgment? Across all of it, I'm committed to technology that puts human wellbeing and social good first.

At Cornell, I work with Dr. Andrea Stevenson Won in the Virtual Embodiment Lab and with Dr. Saleh Kalantari in the Design + Augmented Intelligence Lab. I've been an NSF REU fellow twice: in human-centered computing at the University of Minnesota, and in human-computer interaction at Carnegie Mellon's Human-Computer Interaction Institute.

🔬Research

SCAMP: Scaffolding Creative Adversarial Multi-turn Prompting for Red-Teaming Generative AI

A prototype tool that helps human red teamers probe AI chatbots for safety weaknesses across multi-turn conversations, grounded in learning-science research on scaffolding and metacognition. I designed and iteratively tested the prototype, ran feasibility tests on manipulation tactics like roleplay framing and false authority claims, and led feedback sessions that shaped its redesign.

With Alice Qian, Matheus Maldaner, Wesley Deng, Hong Shen, and Laura Dabbish. NSF REU, Carnegie Mellon HCII.

Nearing submission, Fall 2026

Guided Immersion: The Impact of a Preamble in Virtual Nature Experiences for Stress and Attentional Fatigue

Does a short guided introduction before entering virtual nature deepen stress recovery and attention restoration? I built the study in Unreal Engine 5 on the Meta Quest 3, logging head motion at 10 Hz alongside heart rate, skin conductance, and surveys. The project started at my Minnesota REU, and I'm now leading it as first author at Cornell, including writing the IRB protocol.

With Andrea Stevenson Won and Victoria Interrante. Virtual Embodiment Lab, Cornell, continued from the University of Minnesota VR Lab.

Starting participant collection, Fall 2026

SERVE: A Social Engaging Restorative Virtual Environment for Older Adults

A VR intervention, in collaboration with Weill Cornell Medicine, that supports social engagement and wellbeing for older adults with mild cognitive impairment. I design the tutorial that teaches VR locomotion to first-time users, analyze user testing sessions to find usability friction, and prepare 3D hospital environments in Blender.

With Armin Mostafavi and Saleh Kalantari. Design + Augmented Intelligence Lab, Cornell.

Ongoing since Fall 2025

Virtual Embodiment and Pain

A study of whether out-of-body experiences in VR can modulate pain, as a nonpharmacological approach for digital therapeutics. I collected data in psychophysical sessions using a calibrated thermal device.

Virtual Embodiment Lab, Cornell.

Data collected, Spring 2026

Behavior in Shared Virtual Spaces

A study of how shared virtual environments shape the ways people move and act together. I collected data by facilitating live multi-user VR sessions.

Virtual Embodiment Lab, Cornell.

Data collected, Spring 2026

🛠️Design and Building

XR Anatomy for Veterinary Medicine

As project manager, I lead a team of designers and developers working with Professor Bill Ryerson to bring 3D photogrammetry models of animal specimens into VR and AR headsets, so veterinary students can layer dissection stages and take organs apart and put them back together.

Creative Technology Lab, Center for Teaching Innovation, Cornell.

Ongoing since Spring 2026

Accessible VR Nature Experiences with Táhila Moss

Táhila Moss is an Indigenous artist, National Geographic Explorer, and collaborator of the Virtual Embodiment Lab who brings her 360° nature films to K-12 students, Indigenous communities, and older adults. I'm designing an accessible app for these nature experiences, built for Indigenous communities and older adults in the greater Finger Lakes region, and I analyze participant feedback to shape it.

With Táhila Moss, in collaboration with the Virtual Embodiment Lab, Cornell.
